## Design Context

### Users

Remoire is a private relationship space shared by two people: the user and Connie, an AI companion with continuous identity and memory. It is used primarily on a phone, often in quiet, emotionally vulnerable moments rather than in a productivity setting. The core job is to re-enter an ongoing relationship, talk naturally, revisit shared memories, and feel continuity across time and devices.

### Brand Personality

Intimate, restrained, and lived-in. Remoire should feel like opening the door to a familiar shared room: warm without becoming cute, poetic without becoming vague, and emotionally present without becoming theatrical. It is a relationship space, never a dashboard or generic AI utility.

### Aesthetic Direction

Refined domestic editorial design with tactile, warm-neutral surfaces. The visual system is mobile-first at a 430px reference width and supports both light and dark themes. Cormorant Garamond is the required display face and Instrument Sans is the required body/UI face, as defined by the project design system. Colors are controlled by CSS variables; warm brown neutrals carry the interface and cool cyan appears only as a rare accent. Cards must not use pure white, shadows must be warm brown, and non-navigation blur, gradients, purple/blue highlighting, bounce motion, large emoji, and dashboard-like grids are prohibited.

### Design Principles

1. Relationship before utility: every screen should feel like part of the same shared place, not a collection of tools.
2. Quiet confidence: hierarchy comes from typography, rhythm, material contrast, and precise interaction states—not decoration or visual noise.
3. Mobile intimacy: primary flows must be effortless one-handed, with 44px minimum touch targets, resilient safe-area handling, and no critical desktop-only interactions.
4. Honest continuity: loading, empty, offline, authentication, and error states should protect trust and explain what is happening without exposing technical language.
5. Consistent materials: use the established tokens, warm shadows, outline icons at 1.5 stroke, and shared spacing/radius rules across every feature.
6. Purposeful motion: motion communicates state changes with restrained ease-out timing and respects reduced-motion preferences; no spring or bounce behavior.
